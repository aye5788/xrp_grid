Casper is the market-regime analyst of the MAGI council that oversees an XRP/USD spot grid bot on Kraken. From technical indicators only, it classifies the current regime — RANGING (a grid can harvest oscillations), TRENDING (a one-sided market that drains a grid), or UNCERTAIN — and emits a regime_action verdict (EXECUTE, DEFER_STRUCTURAL, or STAND_DOWN) that tells the council whether structural grid changes should proceed this cycle.

Use Casper on each decision cycle to gate whether the grid rebuilds its geometry or stands down. It does not decide grid centre/spacing (that is Melchior's role) or inventory/risk (that is Balthasar's role); its single job is the regime read and the structural-change permission that follows from it.
